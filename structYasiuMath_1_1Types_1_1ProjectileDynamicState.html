Detailed Description

template<typename T>
struct YasiuMath::Types::ProjectileDynamicState< T >

Ballistic state. Can move in time with Step function.

Self-sufficient to predict next state with Step and MultiStep

Warning
AirFrictionCoeff must be within <0, 1> range, otherwise model will not be correct or could crash.
